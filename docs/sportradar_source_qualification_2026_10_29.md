**Status: research only.** The corrected `SPORTRADAR_API_KEY` works, but the retained and newly sampled pages do not establish a complete, compatible, time-legal current input stream for the frozen predictor. This audit produced no new formal forward observation, betting recommendation, or approved source substitution.

## Source overlap and calculator sensitivity

In the saved ATP overlap, 323 matches yielded 622 comparable player-stat rows. Exact agreement between the pinned history and Sportradar was 613/622 aces, 620/622 double faults, 595/622 service points, 380/622 first serves in, 444/622 first-serve points won, and 448/622 second-serve points won. Eight provider rows fail internal serve arithmetic and were quarantined, including seven split-versus-aggregate conflicts and one impossible second-serve count. Among the other 614 rows, mean absolute vendor differences were 0.875 percentage points for first-serve-in rate and 0.858 points for second-serve win rate; both had a 3.571-point 95th percentile.

The retrospective ATP replay substituted 303 arithmetically admissible provider matches into otherwise pinned history. Across 473 later archived targets, frozen Strict Core probabilities changed on all 473: mean absolute movement **0.0371 percentage points**, 95th percentile **0.1620**, maximum **0.4049**, with one favorite flip. Six date-stratified targets through the final frozen stack moved by a mean **0.1072 points**, maximum **0.4163**, with no flips. These are sensitivity measurements using later-observed historical data, not forecast accuracy or source approval.

A further offline match-level reconciliation found **21/311** matched ATP events where the two players' `points_won` sum disagrees with their combined service-point totals; these are exactly the 21 events whose provider service-point total differs from the pinned record. For example, closed Brisbane event `sr:sport_event:67291728` reports 116 combined match points but only 52 combined service points, although its final score is 6-2 6-4. The new fail-closed intake check rejects this partial line when all four totals are present. Among the other matched ATP events, **155/311** still have at least one differing first-serve split despite equal player service-point denominators. The retained Australian Open Wawrinka–Gea line, for example, has 139/176 first serves in for Wawrinka, while the [Australian Open match page](https://ausopen.com/match/2026-arthur-gea-vs-stan-wawrinka-ms219) and pinned history report 109/176. The remaining split differences are not resolved by the conservation check.

A [five-match Australian Open triangulation](notes/ao-serve-source-arbitration-2026-10-29.md) now confirms the pinned source against tournament match-centre data on all 60 checked serve-field values. Sportradar agrees on all ten player rows for aces, double faults and service-point denominators, but on only one row for each of the first/second-serve split fields. This is a source-admission failure for those AO split fields, not a representative estimate for other seasons.

One [WTA Rome season-page request](https://github.com/AidanDCM/Tennis-Genome-/actions/runs/40918015598) returned all 133 summaries in artifact `12916382496` (SHA-256 `34c4c81711ce262eed3866767daa78d21691aef50f9bc688b236cd781d10598b`). Ninety-four of 95 pinned Rome matches joined; one abnormal match was excluded. In 186 player rows, exact agreement was 185/186 aces, 183/186 double faults, 175/186 service points, 102/186 first serves in, 128/186 first-serve points won, and 137/186 second-serve points won. Three provider events failed internal arithmetic checks. Replacing the remaining 90 matched Rome records changed frozen Core probability for 50 of 178 later archived WTA targets (mean absolute movement **0.0224 points**, maximum **0.7181**, no flips). Three fixed date-stratified final-stack targets were unaffected because their players had no exposure to the swapped history. A separately selected maximum-Core-shift stress case moved **0.6168 points** through the final stack, without a favorite flip.

Across the complete retained ATP census, 70/1,505 closed matches with two stat rows and all four necessary totals fail match-point conservation. The sampled Rome page has 13/131 such failures. These are source-quality counts, not extra independent match-prediction observations. Some gaps are only one point and need provider review; the rule conservatively excludes any contradiction rather than guessing how to repair it.

Sportradar's [season-summary field reference](https://developer.sportradar.com/tennis/reference/season-summaries) defines the serve fields used here. Its [tennis FAQ](https://developer.sportradar.com/tennis/reference/faq) distinguishes official ATP tournament data from independently collected WTA data. Neither fact establishes which source is correct where counts differ. The [coverage documentation](https://developer.sportradar.com/tennis/docs/tennis-ig-data-coverage-tiers) also makes match-level detailed serve coverage conditional.

## Decision and next gate

Keep the frozen predictor, recorded predictions, and registered experiments unchanged. Do not silently combine point statistics from these sources or spend the 46-request lower bound as a blind bulk job. First resolve the conflicting serve definitions and revisions on a small matched sample, verify provider-to-canonical identities and surface/reference fields, then run a capped, checkpointed capture with a dated complete-season inventory. Every admitted input still needs its own pre-start chronology and provenance check. Research captures and retrospective replays remain separate from formal forward evidence.

The one-request raw-capture workflow was added in [PR #327](https://github.com/AidanDCM/Tennis-Genome-/pull/327), serve arithmetic validation in [PR #328](https://github.com/AidanDCM/Tennis-Genome-/pull/328), and the bounded season-page option in [PR #329](https://github.com/AidanDCM/Tennis-Genome-/pull/329). This note retains identifiers and hashes, not vendor data or secret values. GitHub workflow artifacts have finite retention; a durable, licensed evidence archive would be needed before relying on them for long-term reproduction.
