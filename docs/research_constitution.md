# Tennis Genome Research Constitution

Status: **executable governance contract for new Workbench canonical evidence**

The Research Constitution is intentionally small. It protects evidence boundaries that
should not change merely because a new modeling idea is attractive.

Canonical Workbench evaluation identity now binds the exact constitution SHA-256 alongside
the procedure, evaluation, dataset, code, runtime, exposure graph and search family.

## Required invariants

1. T0 information only.
2. Independent tennis probability research remains market-blind.
3. Proper scores are primary probability metrics.
4. Protected data are single-use scientific assets.
5. Descendant procedures inherit research exposure.
6. Canonical evidence binds exact data, code, runtime and specification.
7. The search family that produced a result must be declared.
8. Negative evidence is permanent.
9. Failed prospective hypotheses remain failed.
10. Frozen v1 production is isolated from v2 research.
11. Complex procedures compete with simpler alternatives.
12. Predictive success is not market-edge evidence.
13. Market-edge evidence is not realized profitability.
14. Prospective cohorts require denominator accounting.

## Research authority

The constitution also makes explicit what Workbench research cannot authorize:

- modification of frozen v1;
- bypass of T0 legality;
- bypass of protected-data burn;
- omission of search attempts from family accounting;
- market-data consumption inside the independent probability lane;
- market-edge claims based only on predictive evidence;
- wagering activation;
- real-money risk changes.

A future constitution may add rules or tighten them, but any material change requires a
new constitution identity/version and therefore changes canonical evaluation identity.

The constitution is not intended to encode preferred model families, feature choices,
hyperparameters, or research conclusions. Those belong in procedure/evaluation/search
specifications, not permanent governance.
