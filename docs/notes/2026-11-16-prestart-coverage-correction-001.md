# November 16 coverage correction

This append-only correction supplements [the November 16 capture snapshot](2026-11-16-prestart-coverage-001.md). It does not change a prediction or turn retrospective discovery into a pre-match forecast.

The [official WTA Colina order of play](https://wtafiles.wtatennis.com/pdf/draws/2026/2076/OP.pdf), observed on November 16, lists **eight** November 16 WTA 125 singles matches across Center Court and Grandstand. The PDF prints a November 15, 18:29 release time. We did not find this document during the earlier capture; its exact accessibility at that observation is unverified. The prior snapshot therefore counted two dated ATP slots but left these eight WTA slots unresolved. With this later-discovered source, the November 16 scheduled singles denominator is at least ten (two ATP, eight WTA), subject to later schedule changes and exclusions.

This was a source-discovery failure. It did not cause a missed *valid* frozen forecast to be backfilled: qualified current-player state and a supported WTA Web Shadow route for Colina were absent. The eight WTA fixtures must remain unpredicted. Future WTA schedule searches should inspect the official event order-of-play PDF, not only the draw or tournament overview, and retain its observation time before any start claim.
